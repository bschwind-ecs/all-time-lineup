All-Time Lineup Builder

Type a name. Press Enter or tap a result to add him to the highlighted slot, or to the first open slot that matches his position.
Add a player manually (own photo or cutout)
About, credits and licenses

Tap or click a slot to pick who goes there, drag players between slots to swap, and hit the x to remove. Use Screenshot view for a clean image to share. Your lineup autosaves in this browser only.

About this site

A fan-made tool for building all-time lineups. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NFL, any team or any player. Player names are used only to identify the players you pick.

Your data

There are no accounts, ads or tracking here. Your lineup is saved only in your own browser and is never sent to a server.
