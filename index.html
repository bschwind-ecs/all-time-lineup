About, credits and licenses · v5

Tap or click a slot to pick who goes there, drag players between slots to swap, and hit the x to remove. The number on each player is his rating; red means he is playing out of position. Use Screenshot view for a clean image to share. Your lineup autosaves in this browser only.

About this site

A fan-made tool for building all-time lineups. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the NFL, any team or any player. Player names are used only to identify the players you pick.

Your data

There are no accounts, ads or tracking here. Your lineup is saved only in your own browser and is never sent to a server.

Ratings

Each player gets a 50 to 99 rating from Pro Bowls, first-team All-Pro selections, the Hall of Fame, career Approximate Value and seasons played. A player put at a position he did not play loses points. The team rating is the average of the 11 on the field. It is a formula, not gospel, so argue away.

Pro Bowls, All-Pros and Approximate Value come from Pro Football Reference via nflverse for players drafted since 1980. For older and undrafted players these counts were entered by hand and may be off by one here and there. Players whose careers ended before 1974 come from a hand-entered list of legends. Corrections are welcome through the link below.

Daily challenge

One rule a day, the same for everyone, picked by the date. Only players in the built-in list count, since they are the ones with stats on file. Your results stay in your browser.

Photos and descriptions

Photos and short descriptions are loaded live from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata. Each photo belongs to its photographer and has its own license, such as CC BY-SA, CC BY or public domain. To see the author and license of a photo, open the player's Wikipedia article below and click the image.
